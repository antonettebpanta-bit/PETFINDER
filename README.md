PETFINDER – Responsible Pet Adoption & Rehoming Guide
Helping Every Pet Find the Care They Deserve.

Project Overview
PETFINDER is a multi-page informational website developed as an academic project to promote responsible pet adoption, safe rehoming practices, and proper pet care in the Philippines.

The website aims to provide accessible and practical educational resources for pet owners who experience difficulties providing consistent care due to financial limitations, demanding schedules, or managing multiple pets. It also assists prospective adopters in understanding the responsibilities associated with pet ownership.

Project Objectives
Educate pet owners about responsible and safe rehoming practices.

Encourage owners to explore alternatives before surrendering their pets.

Guide prospective adopters in preparing for pet ownership.

Promote responsible daily pet care and long-term commitment.

Provide links to animal welfare organizations in the Philippines.

Website Pages
1. Home
 Introduces the website’s purpose and provides navigation to the educational guides.

2. Rehoming Your Pet
 Discusses alternatives to rehoming, responsible caregiver screening, and safe handover procedures.

3. How to Adopt
 Provides information about adoption readiness, preparation, budgeting, and long-term responsibilities.

4. Responsible Pet Care
 Presents essential pet care information, including nutrition, hygiene, health, exercise, and managing multiple pets.

5. Resources & About
 Explains the project’s mission and provides links to Philippine animal welfare organizations.

Academic Disclaimer
PETFINDER is an unofficial student project developed for academic and educational purposes only. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Petfinder.

The website does not facilitate actual pet adoption or rehoming transactions, provide live pet listings, or collect adoption applications.

Information presented on the website is intended for general educational purposes and should not replace professional veterinary or legal advice.
